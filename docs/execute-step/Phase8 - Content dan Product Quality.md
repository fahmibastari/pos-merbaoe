# PHASE 8 — CONTENT & PRODUCT QUALITY

Audit seluruh copywriting/UI text:

- terminology;
- consistency;
- capitalization;
- error messages;
- button labels;
- form instructions;
- confirmation dialogs;
- empty states;
- success messages.

Pastikan bahasa:

- ringkas;
- natural;
- kontekstual;
- tidak teknis untuk end-user;
- konsisten.
